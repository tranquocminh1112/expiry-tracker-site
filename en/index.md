## Frequently asked questions

**What's the fastest way to add an item?**
Type a line like `Noodles 7 packs 3/22/2027` in the field at the bottom of the screen and tap +. For more detail, tap + in the top corner instead.

**Not seeing notifications?**
Open the app's Settings to turn on notifications and choose a reminder time. If notifications are off at the system level, open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Use-By.

**How do I use, discard, or edit an item?**
Swipe an item left or right, or **long-press** it to see every action: Edit, Use 1, Use up, Discard (including items that spoiled early), Delete.

**I tapped "Use 1" or "Use up" by mistake?**
Tap "Undo" right after, or open the item → History → Undo.

**Can I share the list with my household?**
Settings → "Invite household members" → send the link via Messages or WhatsApp. The invitee needs an iPhone signed in to iCloud. For each person you choose **Can edit** or **View only**. View-only members can't change anything but still get reminders.

**Can I have more than one list?**
Tap the list name at the top of the screen ("Home ⌄") to switch lists or create a new one. Each list is shared separately with different people.

**Can I filter the list by category?**
When a list holds items from two or more categories, a row of categories appears under the list name, with a count for each. Tap one to see only those items, or "All" to see everything again. As you type a name, the app suggests a category ("Is it …?"). The suggestion runs on your iPhone and the name isn't sent anywhere.

**How do I change the category of several items at once?**
Tap the list name at the top of the screen → "Select Items", pick the items, then tap "Change Category…". A category belongs to the item name, so items with the same name in that list change too.

**How do I record a voucher's value, a SIM balance or points?**
For vouchers, loyalty points and SIM & plans, open the item and fill in "Value", or type it when you add the item: `Gift card $50 12/20/2026`. If points expire in several batches, add each batch as an item with the same name; the app adds them up as "Total held".

**Can I move items to another list, or merge lists?**
Long-press an item → "Move to List…". To move a whole list, tap the list name → "Merge into Another List…"; every item goes across with its quantity and history. The app never deletes the old list on its own; it asks you separately. Family members still on an older version keep using the shared list as before, and need the update to see categories and values.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
