# Nhắc hạn đồ ăn (Food Expiration Reminder)

Track the expiry dates of food at home. The app reminds you monthly starting 12 months before expiry, daily in the final week, sends a weekly digest, and can be shared with your household via iCloud.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the fastest way to add an item?**
Type a line like `Cá kho 7 gói 22/3/2027` in the field at the bottom of the screen and tap +. For more detail, tap + in the top corner instead.

**Not seeing notifications?**
Open the app's Settings to turn on notifications and choose a reminder time. If notifications are off at the system level, open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Nhắc hạn.

**How do I use, discard, or edit an item?**
Swipe an item left or right, or **long-press** it to see every action: Edit, Use 1, Use up, Discard (including items that spoiled early), Delete.

**I tapped "Use 1" or "Use up" by mistake?**
Tap "Undo" right after, or open the item → History → Undo.

**Can I share the list with my household?**
Settings → "Invite household members" → send the link via Messages or Zalo. The invitee needs an iPhone signed in to iCloud. For each person you choose **Can edit** or **View only**. View-only members can't change anything but still get reminders.

**Can I have more than one list?**
Tap the list name at the top of the screen ("Nhà mình ⌄") to switch lists or create a new one. Each list is shared separately with different people.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
